One line a gamer texts the boss to get out of work during GTA 6 launch week. No cliches the tired manager has already heard twice — no "sick", no "family emergency", no "car broke down", no "internet down". Instead, one small concrete detail that makes it feel real: a dentist who moved a filling to 9:40 this morning, and a patient who can't swap the appointment again. Casual, slightly apologetic, no over-explaining — the kind of mundane, verifiable-sounding text a real person sends in a hurry, and the kind a manager almost believes.
